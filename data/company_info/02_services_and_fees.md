# Services and Fees

## Services we offer

1. Property sales: listing, marketing and negotiating residential sales.
2. Property purchase: finding properties that match a buyer's budget and area.
3. Rentals: landlord listings and tenant search.
4. Property management for rented homes: rent collection and maintenance
   coordination.
5. Documentation support: transfer letters, mutation and registry assistance.

## Our fees

### Sales
Commission is 1 percent of the sale price from the buyer and 1 percent from the
seller. This is the standard rate across Islamabad and Rawalpindi.

### Rentals
One month's rent as commission, split equally: half paid by the landlord, half
paid by the tenant.

### Property management
5 percent of the monthly rent, charged to the landlord.

### Viewings
Property viewings are free. There is no charge to see a property, and no
registration fee to become a client.

## Payment terms

Commission becomes payable when the transfer is completed at the relevant
authority, not before. We do not take advance commission.

We accept bank transfer and cheque. We do not accept cash payments above
PKR 50,000 for commission, in line with our internal policy.

## Documentation we help with

- Sale agreement drafting
- Transfer letter and mutation at CDA, RDA or the relevant society office
- Token and bayana receipts
- Rent agreement on stamp paper, registered where required
- Police verification paperwork for tenants
