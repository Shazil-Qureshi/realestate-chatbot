# How Viewings and Transactions Work

## Booking a viewing

1. Tell us the property reference code, or describe what you are looking for:
   area, budget, number of bedrooms.
2. We confirm availability with the owner.
3. We agree a time. Viewings run Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM, and
   on Sunday by prior appointment only.
4. One of our agents meets you at the property.

We usually confirm a viewing within 24 hours of your request. Same-day viewings
are often possible for properties in Bahria Town and DHA Phase 2.

## What to bring

For a viewing, bring a CNIC. Nothing else is required.

## Making an offer

1. You tell us your offer. We present it to the owner.
2. If accepted, a token amount is paid to hold the property, usually
   PKR 100,000 to PKR 500,000 depending on the property value.
3. A sale agreement is signed and bayana (typically 10 to 25 percent of the
   price) is paid.
4. The balance is paid at transfer, usually within 30 to 60 days.

## Renting process

1. Viewing.
2. Offer and agreement on rent and advance.
3. Advance is normally 2 to 3 months' rent, held as security.
4. Rent agreement signed, usually for 11 months.
5. Police verification of the tenant is arranged.
6. Possession handed over.

## Cancellations

If a buyer withdraws after paying bayana, the bayana is usually forfeited to
the seller. If a seller withdraws, the buyer is normally entitled to double the
bayana back. The exact terms are written into each sale agreement.
