# About Bella Real Estate

Bella Real Estate is a residential property agency operating in Islamabad and
Rawalpindi, Pakistan. We help clients buy, sell and rent homes: houses,
apartments and portions.

Note: Bella Real Estate is a fictional company created for a software
demonstration. The company, its staff, its offices and every property listed
are invented. No real property is offered for sale or rent.

## Founded

Bella Real Estate was founded in 2014 and has completed over 1,200 residential
transactions.
