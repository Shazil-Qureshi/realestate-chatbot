# Frequently Asked Questions — Buying

## How much deposit do I need to reserve a property?
A token amount of PKR 100,000 to PKR 500,000 reserves a property, depending on
its value. Bayana of 10 to 25 percent follows when the sale agreement is
signed.

## How long does a purchase take to complete?
Usually 30 to 60 days from the signed sale agreement to transfer, depending on
how quickly the society or authority processes the transfer.

## What is your commission on a purchase?
1 percent of the sale price from the buyer, payable when the transfer is
completed.

## Can I negotiate the listed price?
Yes. Listed prices are asking prices. We present every reasonable offer to the
owner.

## Do you verify that the documents are genuine?
Yes. Before a sale agreement is signed we check the transfer letter or registry,
confirm the seller's identity against CNIC, and verify with the society or
authority that no dues are outstanding and the file is clear.

## What extra costs should I budget for beyond the price?
Transfer fee charged by the society or authority, stamp duty, our 1 percent
commission, and any outstanding utility dues cleared at handover. As a rough
guide, budget 3 to 5 percent of the purchase price for these combined.

## Can overseas Pakistanis buy through you?
Yes. We work with overseas clients regularly. A relative or a lawyer with power
of attorney can complete the transfer on your behalf.

## Do you sell plots?
We deal in residential property with construction on it: houses, apartments and
portions. We do not list empty plots, commercial property or agricultural land.
