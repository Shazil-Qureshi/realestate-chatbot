# Frequently Asked Questions — Renting

## How much advance is required?
Normally 2 to 3 months' rent as a security deposit. The exact amount is listed
with each property.

## Is the advance refundable?
Yes. The advance is returned at the end of the tenancy after deducting any
unpaid utility bills and the cost of repairing damage beyond normal wear.

## How long is a standard rent agreement?
11 months. Most landlords renew with an annual increase of 10 percent, which is
written into the agreement.

## What is your commission for renting?
One month's rent, split equally between landlord and tenant. So a tenant pays
half a month's rent as commission.

## Is police verification required?
Yes. Tenant police verification is required in both Islamabad and Rawalpindi.
We arrange it as part of our service at no extra charge.

## Do you rent to bachelors or students?
It depends on the landlord. Some properties are listed as family only, and this
is noted in the listing. Tell us your situation and we will show you properties
whose owners accept it.

## Who pays for maintenance during a tenancy?
Structural repairs and major systems are the landlord's responsibility. Minor
day-to-day maintenance and the cost of damage caused by the tenant are the
tenant's responsibility. The split is written into each agreement.

## Are utility bills included in the rent?
No. Electricity, gas, water and internet are paid by the tenant unless a
listing states otherwise.
