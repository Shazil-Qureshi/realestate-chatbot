# Frequently Asked Questions — General

## What are your opening hours?
Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Sunday is closed for walk-ins, but
viewings can be arranged by appointment.

## Where are your offices?
Head office: Office 304, Silver Square Plaza, F-11 Markaz, Islamabad.
Branch: Shop 12, Rania Mall, Bahria Town Phase 7, Rawalpindi.

## Which areas do you cover?
Islamabad: F-6, F-7, F-8, F-10, F-11, E-11, G-11, G-13, DHA Phase 2, Bahria
Town Islamabad and Gulberg Greens. Rawalpindi: Bahria Town Phase 4, 7 and 8,
Satellite Town and Chaklala Scheme 3.

## Do you charge for viewings?
No. Viewings are free and there is no registration fee.

## How quickly can I see a property?
We usually confirm a viewing within 24 hours. Same-day viewings are often
possible in Bahria Town and DHA Phase 2.

## Can you help me sell my property?
Yes. We photograph and list the property, market it, handle enquiries and
negotiate on your behalf. Our commission is 1 percent of the sale price.

## How do I contact a human agent?
Call 051-111-2222 for Islamabad or 051-333-4444 for Rawalpindi during opening
hours, or leave your name and phone number here and an agent will call you back.

## Do you handle commercial property?
No. We deal in residential property only.
