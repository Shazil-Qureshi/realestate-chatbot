Services & fees

No surprises: commission is payable only when the transfer is completed, never in advance.

Buying & selling

1% + 1%

1 percent of the sale price from the buyer and 1 percent from the seller. Standard across Islamabad and Rawalpindi.

Rentals

1 month's rent

Split equally — half paid by the landlord, half by the tenant.

Property management

5% monthly

5 percent of the monthly rent, charged to the landlord. Rent collection and maintenance coordination.

Viewings

Free

No charge to see a property, and no registration fee to become a client.

We also help with documentation: sale agreements, transfer and mutation at CDA, RDA or the relevant society office, rent agreements on stamp paper, and tenant police verification paperwork.
